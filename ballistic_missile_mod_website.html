// LAUNCH SEQUENCE PROTOCOL

HOW IT WORKS

Deploy coordinate-targeted strikes in four operational steps, plus optional automated airspace defense.

01
Step 01

Build the 3x3 Launch Pad

Assemble the 3x3 multiblock Launch Pad structure on a clear firing site with unobstructed sky access.

02
Step 02

Place a Missile

Mount either a Standard Missile for medium-range tactical strikes or a Heavy Missile for high-yield strategic devastation onto the pad.

03
Step 03

Enter Coordinates

Open the Coordinate Terminal's interactive screen and input your target coordinates.

04
Step 04

Launch

Initiate ignition. Watch the physics-driven missile ascend and travel along its ballistic trajectory toward impact.

Optional Defense Step // COUNTER-BATTERY

Deploy an Anti-Air System

Protect your base by setting up an automated Anti-Air System stocked with Interceptor Missiles. It automatically fires to shoot down incoming threats—offering strong protection against Standard Missiles, though it struggles against the extreme terminal descent speed of Heavy Missiles.

// TACTICAL DOCTRINE & TIPS

Anti-Air System vs. Heavy Missile Dynamics

Understanding the interaction between Anti-Air Systems and the two offensive missile classes is critical for both base defense and strategic strikes:

Against Standard Missiles

The Anti-Air System is very effective against Standard Missiles. Because Standard Missiles operate on a medium-range tactical profile, automated Interceptor Missiles can reliably track and shoot them down before impact.

Against Heavy Missiles

The Anti-Air System struggles against the extreme descent speed of Heavy Missiles. Because a Heavy Missile climbs to high altitude and plunges down in a steep, high-velocity terminal descent, interceptors have a narrow reaction window to stop it before detonation.
